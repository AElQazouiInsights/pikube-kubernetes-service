10) Notes on safety and rollbacks
- SUC Plans track v1.34 channel; they won’t upgrade across minors unless you change the channel to `stable`.
- Use `sudo k3s etcd-snapshot save` before upgrades; keep recent snapshots off‑node.
- Rollback path: patch `spec.version` or change `channel` to a previous minor track if needed; SUC will reconcile.
